How to clean your steel bottle: 4 simple tips

A clean bottle keeps your water fresh. These habits take only a few minutes.

1. Wash it every day

Use warm water and a drop of mild soap. Scrub the inside and the lid with a bottle brush.

2. Remove smells with baking soda

Soak the bottle for an hour with warm water and a spoon of baking soda, then rinse well.

3. Dry with the lid off

Leave the bottle upside down with the lid open so air can dry the inside.

4. Do not store drinks overnight

Empty and rinse your bottle at the end of the day to keep it fresh for tomorrow.
